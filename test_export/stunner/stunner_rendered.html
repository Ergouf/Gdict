Definition of 'stunner'

Word Frequency

stunner

(stʌnəʳ ) Collins COBUILD
Word forms: plural stunners
1. countable noun
A stunner is an extremely attractive woman.
[informal]
One of the girls was an absolute stunner.
Synonyms: beauty, looker [informal, mainly US], lovely [slang], dish [informal] More Synonyms of stunner
2. countable noun
If you say that something is a stunner, you mean that it is very surprising or impressive.
[informal]
Their debut single is a stunner.
